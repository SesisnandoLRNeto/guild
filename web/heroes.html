The rules of the hall

XP per quest: 20 for setting out, +80 when done, +40 for a pull request, +15 per grade star, +25 for checks green on the first run, +5 per dollar spent. Each level needs a little more than the last. Titles: Recruit, Squire (3), Adventurer (5), Veteran (8), Champion (12), Hero (16), Legend (20).
Stats (1 to 20): STR quests finished, INT your grades, DEX speed, CON how often it finishes, WIS checks green on the first run, CHA how often you took its advice.
